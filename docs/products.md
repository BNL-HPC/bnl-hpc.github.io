# Products

## CelloAI

- **Description:** A locally hosted coding assistant that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to support High Energy Physics (HEP) code documentation and generation.
- **Citation:** Atif, M., Chopra, K., Kilic, O., Wang, T., Dong, Z., Leggett, C., Lin, M., Calafiura, P., & Habib, S. _"CelloAI: Leveraging Large Language Models for HPC Software Development in High Energy Physics."_ [arXiv:2508.16713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16713), 2025.
