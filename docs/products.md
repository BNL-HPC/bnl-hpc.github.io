# Products

## CelloAI

- **Description:** A locally hosted coding assistant that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to support High Energy Physics (HEP) code documentation and generation.
- **References:**
    - [Source Code](https://github.com/hep-cce/celloai)
- **Publications:**
    - Atif, M., Chopra, K., Tsai, F., Kilic, O., Wang, T., Dong, Z., Benjamin, D., Leggett, C., Lin, M., Calafiura, P., & Habib, S. (2026). *CelloAI Benchmarks: Toward Repeatable Evaluation of AI Assistants*. arXiv. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01051](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01051)
    - Atif, M., Chopra, K., Kilic, O., Wang, T., Dong, Z., Leggett, C., Lin, M., Calafiura, P., & Habib, S. (2025). *CelloAI: Leveraging Large Language Models for HPC Software Development in High Energy Physics*. arXiv. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16713)

## SciBot (RHIC Multi-Experiment Chatbot)

- **Description:** A secure, high-performance AI assistant designed for the long-term preservation of RHIC knowledge. Originating from the RHIC Data & Analysis Preservation (DAPP) program, it serves as a central knowledge discovery tool—essentially a "Librarian" for RHIC experiments. Built for adaptability, SciBot is now evolving into a broader community effort that extends to the EIC/ePIC initiatives.
- **References:**
    - [Chatbot Interface](https://scibot.csi.bnl.gov/)
    - [Source Code](https://git.racf.bnl.gov/gitea/DAP/scibot)
