# **Computational Science Department** at Brookhaven National Laboratory

> **Pioneering the next generation of scientific computing, AI-driven data architectures, and high-performance workflows.**

Our research activities bridge the gap between complex physical sciences and cutting-edge computer science:

1. **High-Performance Computing (HPC):** Optimizing high-performance computing software for heterogeneous architectures and exascale supercomputers.
2. **AI for Science:** Developing AI applications for biology, chemistry, physics, and autonomous scientific discovery.
3. **Scientific Computing Infrastructures:** Architecting high-performance infrastructures for large-scale scientific workflows and next-generation experiments.
